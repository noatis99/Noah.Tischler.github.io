About me

As of 09.2024 I am a PhD Student in Mathematics at the College of Doctoral Training Alegbra, Geometry & Quantum Fields, under the supervision of Prof. Richard J. Szabo and Prof. Murad Alim. I am based at Heriot Watt University. My main interests are Enumerative Geometry and Mathematical Physics. In particular, I am curious about Calabi-Yau three- and fourfolds and applying ideas from string theory to study ideal sheaf moduli.
